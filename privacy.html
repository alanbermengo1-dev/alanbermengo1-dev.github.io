Privacy Policy

Last updated: November 14, 2026

Uploader ("the application") is a private tool used by its owner to upload videos to the owner's own YouTube channel. The application uses YouTube API Services.

Information the application accesses

When the channel owner authorizes the application with their Google account, the application receives an OAuth access token and refresh token that allow it to upload videos to that YouTube channel and to set video titles, descriptions and thumbnails. The application does not read, collect or store any other personal data, messages, contacts or viewing history.

How the information is used

The tokens are used only to upload videos prepared by the channel owner to the channel owner's YouTube channel. No data is used for advertising, analytics or profiling.

Storage and sharing

The tokens are stored only on the owner's private server with access restricted to the application. They are not sold, shared with or disclosed to any third party.

Revoking access

Access can be revoked at any time on the Google security settings page: https://myaccount.google.com/permissions. After revocation the stored tokens stop working and are deleted.

YouTube and Google policies

By using this application you agree to be bound by the YouTube Terms of Service. Information handled through YouTube API Services is also subject to the Google Privacy Policy.

Changes

This policy may be updated from time to time. The date at the top of this page shows when it was last changed.
